I exported my bookmarks: 417 links, 356 domains, collected over several years.
Half of it turned out to be rubbish - one-off articles, hosting panels, forgotten
tabs. What follows is what survived the clean-up.

Every entry has a link, a line on what it is for, and a fact that makes it
interesting in its own right. I checked the numbers today rather than copying
them from someone else's list. Every link is alive.

## Colour

[realtimecolors.com](https://realtimecolors.com) - pick a palette and see it on a
finished landing page mock-up rather than on swatches.

<LinkPreview id="https://realtimecolors.com" title="realtimecolors.com" description="pick a palette and see it on a finished landing page mock-up rather than on swatches." />

> It solves the core problem with colour generators: four pretty squares tell you
> almost nothing about how text will read against a background. Here you look
> straight at buttons, headings and small type.

[uicolors.app](https://uicolors.app/create) - turns one hex into a full 50 to 950
scale in Tailwind format.

<LinkPreview id="https://uicolors.app/create" title="uicolors.app" description="turns one hex into a full 50 to 950 scale in Tailwind format." />

> Exactly the format `@theme` expects in Tailwind v4: copy and paste. Building
> that scale by hand takes half an hour and still comes out uneven.

[hypercolor.dev](https://hypercolor.dev) - ready-made gradients as Tailwind
classes.

<LinkPreview id="https://hypercolor.dev" title="hypercolor.dev" description="ready-made gradients as Tailwind classes." />

[grainient.supply](https://grainient.supply) - over a thousand gradients with
grain.

<LinkPreview id="https://grainient.supply" title="grainient.supply" description="over a thousand gradients with grain." />

> The grain is not decoration but a technique: a clean gradient across a large
> block bands because of limited bits per channel, and noise hides it. Apple uses
> the same trick in macOS backgrounds.

[fffuel.co](https://fffuel.co) - SVG generators: waves, grids, blobs, noise.

<LinkPreview id="https://fffuel.co" title="fffuel.co" description="SVG generators: waves, grids, blobs, noise." />
